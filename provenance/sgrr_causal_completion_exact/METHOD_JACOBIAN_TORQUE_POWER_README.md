# Local-Policy Jacobian, Torque Shapley Decomposition, and Event-Aligned Analysis

## 1. Purpose and Evidence Boundary

This module answers three questions:

1. Along real trajectories, how sensitive are joint `j` outputs `K1_j` and `K2_j` to local observations `delta_theta_j` and `theta_dot_j`?
2. How do `K1_j*delta_theta_j` and `K2_j*theta_dot_j` jointly form active control torque after clipping to `+/-9`?
3. How do these patterns change before launch, inside valid rolling pulses, and between pulses as identified by the formal frozen evaluator?

It does not train a policy, run the environment, modify a formal checkpoint, or present local gradients as causal conclusions about rolling. Necessity, sufficiency, and cross-joint physical propagation must be established by closed-loop interventions on the same frozen checkpoints.

## 2. Frozen Objects

- Formal training seeds: `9201-9205`.
- Policies: each seed's `Rroll/checkpoint_1500.pt`.
- Per-joint observation: `[delta_theta, theta_dot]`.
- Per-joint action: `[K1, K2]`.
- Physical K: raw policy action multiplied by `100`.
- Control-boundary active torque: `clip(K1*delta_theta + K2*feedback_gain*theta_dot, -9, 9)`.
- Trajectories: real `.npz` files generated by the new causal-completion experiment and the legacy independent trajectory audit, never rewritten by this script.

Only baseline `C11/Rroll` trajectories from the five formal seeds are accepted. The default condition regular expression is:

```text
^(C11|BASELINE_C11|C11_BASELINE|RROLL_BASELINE|BASELINE_RROLL)$
```

If new traces use another baseline condition name, pass it explicitly with `--baseline-condition-regex`; do not edit the trace.

## 3. Important Architectural Result

The formal actor is not a centralized network reading all 16 whole-body observations. It contains eight local MLPs with no parameter sharing:

```text
[delta_theta_j, theta_dot_j] -> local MLP_j -> [K1_j, K2_j]
```

The direct policy Jacobian is consequently a block-diagonal `16 x 16` matrix with eight `2 x 2` blocks:

- within-joint entries: `8 x 2 x 2 = 32`;
- structural cross-joint zeros: `16 x 16 - 32 = 224`.

Those 224 zeros mean only that the actor does not directly read observations from other joints. They do not imply absence of joint coupling. Cross-joint coupling still propagates through robot geometry, contact, dynamics, and observations at the next time step. In addition, `delta_theta_j` is itself constructed from adjacent particles/joints and therefore contains local physical-neighborhood information.

## 4. Analytic Jacobian

For local network `j`:

```text
h1 = tanh(W0*x + b0)
h2 = tanh(W2*h1 + b2)
K  = 100*(W4[:2]*h2 + b4[:2])
```

The analytic derivative is:

```text
dK/dx = 100 * W4[:2]
              * diag(1-h2^2)
              * W2
              * diag(1-h1^2)
              * W0
```

The output axis is `[K1,K2]` and the input axis is `[delta_theta,theta_dot]`. The script computes this local `2 x 2` matrix at actually visited states and embeds the eight blocks into the full `16 x 16` matrix.

For every seed, joint, K channel, observation channel, and event-aligned bin, it exports median, 25th/75th percentiles (IQR), mean, SD, mean absolute value, positive/negative/near-zero proportions, and sign-consistency rate `max(P[d>tol], P[d<-tol])`.

### Independent Numerical Validation

For every seed, a fixed RNG samples actually visited states. At every sampled state, each of the eight local networks is evaluated with:

1. the analytic chain rule above;
2. PyTorch autograd; and
3. double-precision central finite differences.

`JACOBIAN_VALIDATION_PASS.json` is emitted only when analytic values pass both autograd and finite-difference thresholds and every cross-joint entry in the full `16 x 16` matrix is exactly zero.

## 5. Official Event-Aligned Bins

The module dynamically reads the frozen `evaluate_fast_forward_roll.py` from the formal directory and uses its original thresholds and logic to recompute valid rolling pulses and the formal tail launch associated with a valid pulse from `positions/support_index/contact_strength`.

Bins are:

1. `official_prelaunch`: control steps before the launch matched to the first pulse and outside any valid pulse;
2. `official_rolling_outside_pulse`: steps after launch but outside valid pulses; and
3. `official_pulse_q1 ... q5`: each valid pulse divided into five equal bins by normalized time between its start and end.

These are event-aligned analysis bins, not internal policy states, reward-function phases, or exact phases of the ten physics substeps. Trajectories without a valid pulse are conservatively assigned to prelaunch and never used to fabricate a rolling phase.

## 6. Clipping-Aware Two-Channel Shapley Torque

For every joint and control step:

```text
u1 = K1*delta_theta
u2 = K2*feedback_gain*theta_dot
f(S) = clip(sum of channels in S, -9, 9)
```

The exact two-channel Shapley allocation is:

```text
phi1 = 0.5 * [(f({1})-f({})) + (f({1,2})-f({2}))]
phi2 = 0.5 * [(f({2})-f({})) + (f({1,2})-f({1}))]
```

and satisfies:

```text
phi1 + phi2 = clip(u1+u2, -9, 9)
```

This is preferable to directly comparing `|u1|` with `|u2|` because it symmetrically allocates channel competition under saturation. For every event bin, the script exports K, unclipped channel torque, Shapley torque, saturation rate, and correlations with support/contact diagnostics.

## 7. Active-Control Power Proxy

The script reports:

```text
P1_proxy = phi1*theta_dot
P2_proxy = phi2*theta_dot
```

These quantities compare the direction and relative intensity of active work by the two control channels at the control boundary. They must not be called exact mechanical energy or complete energy consumption because:

- one control step contains ten physics substeps;
- pose, velocity, constraint force, and contact change within a substep;
- the current traces do not record every substep's torque and angular velocity; and
- passive elasticity, damping, friction, and contact work are outside this proxy.

Exact energy conservation or substep work would require a separate high-frequency diagnostic rollout that preserves the policy and channels while recording executed torque and generalized velocity at every physics substep.

## 9. Primary Outputs

Default output directory: `analysis_local_actor`.

- `trace_inventory.csv`: real-trajectory inventory, hashes, and frozen evaluation events.
- `jacobian_local_summary_by_seed_event.csv`: local `2 x 2` derivative statistics by seed/joint/K/observation/event bin.
- `jacobian_full_16x16_by_seed_event.csv`: median full `16 x 16` event-bin matrices with structural-zero flags.
- `jacobian_structural_mask_16x16.csv`: 32 local-block entries and 224 structural cross-joint zeros.
- `jacobian_validation.csv` and `JACOBIAN_VALIDATION_PASS.json`: autograd and finite-difference validation.
- `shapley_torque_power_by_seed_event.csv`: clipping-aware torque and active-power proxy by channel.
- `event_bin_contact_summary.csv`: support, contact, and saturation diagnostics by event bin.
- `ANALYSIS_MANIFEST.json`: checkpoint hashes, trace size, methods, and limitations.
- `figures/`: full Jacobian, local sensitivity, sign consistency, Shapley torque, power proxy, saturation-rate, and validation figures.

## 10. Questions This Module Can and Cannot Answer

It can determine which local observation (`delta_theta` or `theta_dot`) each K responds to most, how response direction and magnitude change across official rolling events, which channel owns clipped active torque, which channel provides positive or negative active-power proxy in each phase, and whether direct cross-joint actor-input coupling exists (the architecture guarantees that it does not).

It cannot by itself establish whether a K is necessary or sufficient for rolling success, the delay before other joints respond through the physical loop after a K intervention, whether a large observed gradient improves launch probability/speed/distance, or exact ten-substep mechanical energy, passive work, or contact work. Those questions require joint interpretation with frozen per-K closed-loop interventions, temporal permutation, dose response, cross-seed results, and contact-propagation analysis.
